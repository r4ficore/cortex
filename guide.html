1. Choose your moment

Home offers five paths. Breathe takes you straight to a breathing practice. Focus, Unwind, Sleep and Energy show sessions for your chosen intention.

Energy includes Prime, Overdrive and Gamma Lift. Prime offers a gentler beginning; the other sessions have a more pronounced sound character. Focus is designed to accompany work on a task.

The library includes all 14 sound sessions, breathing and five programmes. Filter by intention or time, or search to find a session.

2. Make it yours

  1. Sit or lie comfortably. For sound sessions, have stereo headphones ready.
  2. Set a low volume. Under “More options”, you can check the left and right channels separately.
  3. Choose brown, pink or white noise, or no background.
  4. If you like, add one minute of breathing first. You can skip it using the player button.
  5. Press “Begin”. Sound starts only after your action.

Hypnos offers 30, 45 or 90 minutes, or no time limit. Other sessions follow the duration of their protocol.

3. Follow your breath

Choose 1, 3 or 5 minutes. Inhale as the form expands and exhale as it contracts. Phase names and a countdown remain available without animation.

The basic 4–6 rhythm has no breath holds. Box and 4–7–8 include optional holds. Do not force yourself to keep up with the count. Breathe gently and return to your natural rhythm if the exercise feels uncomfortable. NHS guidance

The practice works silently. Enable phase-change sounds in preparation if you like. Where vibration is available, an optional tactile rhythm is also offered. Safari on iPhone does not provide this rhythm; system feedback on switches is a separate feature. Hiding the tab pauses guided breathing; resume when you return.

4. Stay in control

Pause freezes the session. Resume continues from the same point. Finish ends the session and records the time spent. You can browse while listening; the bottom bar takes you back to the player.

In programmes, you start each next step yourself. After a session, you can optionally rate how you feel. History is not a health assessment or medical report.

Use “Session visual” to choose a night visual or completely black screen. Tap the black area or press Escape to reveal controls. Browser bars may remain visible. Your phone may interrupt background audio or playback while locked. If the browser reports an interruption, Cortex pauses the session. Check the player state when you return.

5. Rhythmic light and expanded view

  1. In Focus or Energy preparation, open “Visuals and motion” and choose rhythmic light.
  2. Choose “Adapt to display” to cap the frequency at the measured frame cadence, or “Follow the protocol exactly”. At about 60 frames/s, adaptation allows at most 20 Hz; it does not reproduce 40 Hz. Choose gentle, standard or stronger contrast.
  3. Press “Begin with light”, then “Expand view”. After checking the display, light fades in gently. Controls hide after 3 seconds. Tap the field to restore them; “Hide controls” hides them again. Escape or “Collapse view” returns to the regular player.

Uneven frame timing or hiding the tab stops the light. “Check again” repeats the measurement without restarting audio. In exact mode, an unsupported stage remains unmodulated; you can explicitly choose “Adapt to display”. The Hz value describes the stimulus, not brain activity. System or browser bars may remain visible on a phone.

Comfort and safety

Stop if you feel uncomfortable

Do not use Cortex while driving or doing tasks that need your attention. Start at a low volume. If you experience dizziness, pain, nausea or distress, end the session.

Visuals move gently by default. If you are sensitive to stimulation, choose no animation or reduced motion. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is respected.

Rhythmic light is optional, experimental visual stimulation. Enable it in Focus or Energy session preparation, under “Visuals and motion”. It never starts automatically or runs during breathing. Do not enable it if you have sensitivity to flashing light or photosensitive epilepsy. If you are unsure about your health circumstances, discuss use with a clinician. Information on flashing — W3C

Cortex supports personal rituals and does not replace diagnosis or treatment. You do not need to finish an exercise or choose an intense session.

Data and export

Preferences, history and ratings are stored locally in your browser. Clearing site data can remove them. Download a JSON copy from Settings.

Audio export creates a WAV file on your device. Choose a duration, sound arrangement and background, or download SVG artwork and protocol data as JSON. Rendering can be cancelled. Longer files use more memory.
