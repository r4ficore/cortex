1. Choose your moment

Home offers five paths. Breathe takes you straight to a breathing practice. Focus, Unwind, Sleep and Energy show sessions for your chosen intention.

Energy includes Prime, Overdrive and Gamma Lift. Prime offers a gentler beginning; the other sessions have a more pronounced sound character. Focus is designed to accompany work on a task.

The library includes all 14 sound sessions, breathing and five programmes. Filter by intention or time, or search to find a session.

Daily rituals

Time to focus combines optional breathing with 15 minutes of Deep Focus. A little reset offers about 3 minutes of breathing and an optional background. Let the day go leads through optional breathing into Hypnos. Ritual stages advance automatically; you can pause the whole sequence or skip the opening breath.

Save as my ritual stores a separate setup for each ritual. Begin on Home starts the saved version. The evening ritual ends on a dark screen without a feedback prompt. History records one entry for the whole ritual, excluding pauses and skipped time. Later, mark whether it felt helpful and use Repeat to return to its settings. This is subjective feedback, not a measurement of effectiveness.

2. Make it yours

  1. Sit or lie comfortably. For sound sessions, have stereo headphones ready.
  2. Set a low volume. Under “More options”, you can check the left and right channels separately.
  3. Choose the path soundscape, brown, pink, white or green noise, an ambient drone, or no background.
  4. If you like, add one minute of breathing first. You can skip it using the player button.
  5. Press “Begin”. Sound starts only after your action.

Hypnos offers 30, 45 or 90 minutes, or no time limit. Other sessions follow the duration of their protocol.

3. Follow your breath

Choose about 1, 3 or 5 minutes. The displayed duration includes complete cycles: for example, box breathing lasts 1:04, 3:12 or 5:04. You can stop early at any time. In 4–6 and 4–7–8, inhale as the form expands and exhale as it contracts. Box breathing follows a point around a square: up the left side — inhale, across the top — hold, down the right side — exhale, across the bottom — hold. Each side takes 4 seconds. Phase names and the countdown remain readable without movement.

The basic 4–6 rhythm has no breath holds. Box and 4–7–8 include optional holds. Do not force yourself to keep up with the count. Breathe gently and return to your natural rhythm if the exercise feels uncomfortable. NHS guidance

Optional “Breathing sound” provides soft synthesised air throughout each inhale and exhale, with silence during holds. It is not a recording of a person or a notification tone. “Listen to one cycle” previews the sound before a session. Choose visual guidance, sound, or both. Sound is off by default. The optional background and breathing sound have independent volume controls. The background can remain audible during breath holds. Pause stops both the visual and the sound; resume continues from the same position, including within a hold. Sound is also available for the one-minute breathing introduction. Where vibration is available, an optional tactile rhythm is also offered. Safari on iPhone does not provide this rhythm; system feedback on switches is a separate feature. Hiding the tab pauses visual-only breathing. With breathing sound or a background selected, playback may continue if the system permits. The screen-on option works during breathing and light when the system permits it. Pause, finish and the black screen release the lock.

4. Stay in control

Pause freezes the session. Resume continues from the same point. Finish ends the session and records the time spent. You can browse while listening; the bottom bar takes you back to the player.

In programmes, you start each next step yourself. After a session, you can optionally rate how you feel. History is not a health assessment or medical report.

Use “Session visual” to choose a night visual or completely black screen. Tap the black area or press Escape to reveal controls. Browser bars may remain visible. Your phone may interrupt background audio or playback while locked. If the browser reports an interruption, Cortex pauses the session. Check the player state when you return.

Adjust the sound

Before starting, listen to a twelve-second preview of the main stage. Previews do not add a history entry. Choose “Path soundscape” for a harmonic background matched to the path, or select a noise type. Under “More options”, the Tones and Background sliders let you combine the two layers or mute either one.

Intensity adjusts tone level while preserving frequencies. The breathing guide has its own volume slider; changing it during an exercise or prelude does not change the subsequent sound session’s volume. Previous preferences are retained — choose the new harmonic layer under Background sound.

5. Rhythmic light and expanded view

  1. In Focus or Energy preparation, open “Visuals and motion” and choose rhythmic light.
  2. Choose “Adapt to display” to cap the frequency at the measured frame cadence, or “Follow the protocol exactly”. At about 60 frames/s, adaptation allows at most 20 Hz; it does not reproduce 40 Hz. Choose gentle, standard or stronger contrast.
  3. Press “Begin with light”, then “Expand view”. After checking the display, light fades in gently. Controls hide after 3 seconds. Tap the field to restore them; “Hide controls” hides them again. Escape or “Collapse view” returns to the regular player.

Uneven frame timing pauses the light and automatically starts a fresh measurement. Light fades back in after timing stabilises. Hiding the tab requires an explicit restart. “Check again” repeats the measurement without restarting audio. In exact mode, an unsupported stage remains unmodulated; you can explicitly choose “Adapt to display”. The Hz value describes the stimulus, not brain activity. System or browser bars may remain visible on a phone.

Comfort and safety

Stop if you feel uncomfortable

Do not use Cortex while driving or doing tasks that need your attention. Start at a low volume. If you experience dizziness, pain, nausea or distress, end the session.

Visuals move gently by default. If you are sensitive to stimulation, choose no animation or reduced motion. Your system’s reduced-motion preference is respected.

Rhythmic light is optional, experimental visual stimulation. Enable it in Focus or Energy session preparation, under “Visuals and motion”. It never starts automatically or runs during breathing. Do not enable it if you have sensitivity to flashing light or photosensitive epilepsy. If you are unsure about your health circumstances, discuss use with a clinician. Information on flashing — W3C

Cortex supports personal rituals and does not replace diagnosis or treatment. You do not need to finish an exercise or choose an intense session.

On your phone and offline

Open the published Cortex app while online. Check Settings for “Ready for offline use”. The app, locally generated sounds and guide will then work without a connection. External source links still need internet. Initial resource storage requires HTTPS or localhost; a home-network HTTP address is only a preview.

On iPhone, open the page in Safari, choose Share and Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the browser’s installation menu or the button in Cortex when available. The browser may remove stored resources when clearing data or under storage pressure. An update waits until all Cortex windows are closed and does not replace a player during a session.

Data and export

Preferences, history and ratings are stored locally in your browser. Clearing site data can remove them. Download a JSON backup or import one from another device in Settings. Import previews new entries and keeps existing ones; transferring preferences is optional. An active session saves its position every five seconds and when pausing or leaving. On return, choose to resume or save the elapsed time. Sound never starts automatically, and rhythmic light stays off.

Audio export creates a WAV file on your device. Choose a duration, sound arrangement and background, or download SVG artwork and JSON describing the chosen export. Hypnos scales its complete 30-minute edition; steady export uses the main phase. Rendering can be cancelled. 24 kHz WAV files are half the size of 48 kHz files. Supported browsers stream to disk; other browsers limit files to 192 MiB. For larger exports, shorten the duration or choose 24 kHz.
