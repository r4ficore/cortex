Cortex in-app session descriptions — 2026-10-19

Scope
14 sound sessions, the breathing entry and its three selectable patterns,
three quick-start rituals and five programmes, in Polish and English.

Content structure
- Library/preparation: a recognisable situation and the intended benefit.
- An optional explanation after Start: why choose it, how the sequence works,
  and a concrete way to use the session.
- Breathing explanations follow the selected 4–6, Box or 4–7–8 pattern.
- Work/evening rituals reuse the explanation for their actual sound session;
  the reset ritual follows its selected breathing pattern.
- Programme steps show the purpose alongside the actual programme duration.
  The programme start precedes the extended outline on mobile.
- Search also covers benefits and practical usage instructions.

Protocol review

rituals.js and the actual playback/transition logic. Stage descriptions use
relative order rather than standalone timings, so scaled programmes remain
accurate. Sleep options retain finite/unlimited endings; programme steps still
require the user to continue. Audio parameters and stimulation remain unchanged.

Editorial basis
The primary-source review in marketing-copy-2026-10-19.txt and the previous audio
audits remain the evidence basis. Rechecked Tharion 2012 (PMID 23029969): daily
practice for a month, selected HRV changes, not a guaranteed immediate effect.
Lane 1998, Orozco Perez 2020 and Mallik & Russo 2022 describe different research
conditions and do not establish unique benefits for each Cortex preset.

Descriptions therefore distinguish intended use (sustained work, planning,
rest, evening wind-down, getting started) and the real signal sequence. They do
not turn a frequency into a proven physiological dose. REM Sculpt is explained
as an evening practice, not REM-stage control. Clinical and methodological
detail stays in existing protocol evidence and the whitepaper.
